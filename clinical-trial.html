Clinical Trial Feasibility Analysis

SQL | SQLite | Python | Tableau

Analyzed healthcare data across patients, conditions, medications, encounters, procedures, observations, and claims to evaluate a potential diabetes-related clinical trial population.

Business Problem

Clinical trial planning depends on understanding whether enough potentially eligible patients exist and what characteristics may affect recruitment. The goal of this analysis was to evaluate patient populations, medical conditions, medication use, utilization, and cost patterns that could support feasibility planning.

Data

The healthcare database included patient, condition, medication, encounter, procedure, observation, and provider information. The analysis focused on identifying and exploring a Type 2 diabetes patient cohort.

Analysis

I used SQL and SQLite to connect healthcare tables, identify the target cohort, analyze diagnoses and medication use, and calculate encounter and cost metrics. Python was used for additional analysis, while Tableau was used to visualize the results.

Key Findings

  • Identified 6 patients in the Type 2 diabetes cohort.
  • The cohort contained 43 medication records.
  • Metformin appeared in 4 of the 6 diabetes patients.
  • Metabolic syndrome and hypertriglyceridemia were present across the identified diabetes cohort.
  • Diabetes patients averaged approximately 151.7 encounters, indicating high healthcare utilization within the cohort.

Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests that feasibility teams should evaluate both patient count and clinical complexity when assessing potential trial populations. Recruitment planning could also consider medication history, comorbid conditions, encounter frequency, and geographic availability before selecting trial sites.
