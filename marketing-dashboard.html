Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

Excel | PivotTables | Data Visualization

Built an Excel dashboard to analyze marketing campaign performance across revenue, cost, profit, conversions, click-through rate, conversion rate, and return on ad spend.

Business Problem

Marketing teams need a clear way to understand which channels and campaigns are producing strong results and where performance may need improvement. The goal of this project was to create a dashboard that summarizes campaign performance and makes key metrics easy to compare.

Data

The dataset contained campaign-level marketing performance data, including revenue, advertising cost, conversions, click-through rate, conversion rate, and return on ad spend.

Analysis

I used Excel to clean and organize the data, calculate performance metrics, create PivotTables, and build visualizations that allow marketing performance to be compared across channels.

Key Findings

  • Total campaign revenue was approximately $361,000.
  • Total campaign cost was approximately $69,300, producing about $291,700 in profit.
  • The campaigns generated 835 conversions.
  • Average click-through rate was approximately 7.12%.
  • Average conversion rate was approximately 29.60%.
  • Average return on ad spend was approximately 5.24.

Business Recommendations

Marketing teams could use the dashboard to compare performance across channels, identify campaigns generating stronger returns, and shift budget toward higher-performing strategies. Lower-performing channels could be reviewed for targeting, messaging, cost, and conversion opportunities.

Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard provides a consolidated view of campaign performance and key marketing KPIs for faster reporting and decision-making.
