Supply Chain Delivery Performance Analysis

SQL | SQLite | Python | Tableau

Analyzed more than 180,000 shipment records to identify delivery performance trends and determine which shipping methods experienced the highest rates of late delivery.

Business Problem

Delivery delays can affect customer satisfaction, operating costs, and overall supply chain performance. The goal of this analysis was to understand where late deliveries were occurring and identify patterns that could help explain delivery performance.

Data

The dataset contained more than 180,000 shipment records with information related to orders, customers, products, delivery status, shipping methods, and supply chain activity.

Analysis

I used SQL and SQLite to explore shipment performance, aggregate delivery results, and compare late-delivery rates across shipping methods. Python was used for additional analysis, while Tableau was used to visualize the findings.

Key Findings

  • First Class shipping had the highest late-delivery rate at approximately 95.3%.
  • Second Class shipping had a late-delivery rate of approximately 76.6%.
  • Same Day shipping had a late-delivery rate of approximately 45.7%.
  • Standard Class had the lowest late-delivery rate among the analyzed shipping modes at approximately 38.1%.

Business Recommendations

The results suggest that shipping method alone should not be assumed to guarantee faster or more reliable delivery. Operations teams could investigate why First Class and Second Class shipments experience substantially higher late-delivery rates and evaluate factors such as carrier performance, fulfillment processes, routing, and promised delivery expectations.
