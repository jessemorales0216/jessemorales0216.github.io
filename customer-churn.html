Customer Churn Analysis

SQL | SQLite

Analyzed 7,043 telecom customers to identify churn patterns related to contract type, customer tenure, and retention risk.

Business Problem

Customer churn can directly affect recurring revenue and long-term customer value. The goal of this analysis was to identify customer groups with higher churn rates and determine which characteristics were most strongly associated with customer retention.

Data

The dataset contained 7,043 telecom customers with information related to account tenure, contract type, services, billing, customer characteristics, and churn status.

Analysis

I used SQL and SQLite to calculate overall churn rates, compare churn across contract types, and group customers by tenure. These comparisons helped identify customer segments associated with higher and lower churn risk.

Key Findings

  • Overall customer churn was approximately 26.5%.
  • Month-to-month customers had the highest churn rate at approximately 42.7%.
  • One-year contract customers had a churn rate of approximately 11.3%.
  • Two-year contract customers had the lowest churn rate at approximately 2.8%.
  • Customers with 0 to 12 months of tenure had a churn rate of approximately 47.4%, compared with approximately 9.5% for customers with 49 or more months of tenure.

Business Recommendations

Retention efforts could focus on newer customers and customers using month-to-month contracts. The company could test onboarding improvements, targeted retention offers, and incentives that encourage customers to move toward longer-term contracts.
